Areas of Clinical & Research Interest

Multidisciplinary Non-Surgical Oncology Subspecialties

Select a subspecialty pillar below to explore Prof. Biswas’s clinical protocols, conformal radiotherapy techniques, systemic chemotherapy integrations, and landmark AIIMS studies.

Head & Neck Radiation & Clinical Oncology

Comprehensive organ-preserving management of squamous cell carcinomas of the oral cavity, oropharynx, larynx, and hypopharynx, as well as rare head and neck extranodal lymphomas and Ewing’s sarcoma family of tumors (ESFT/pPNET). Combines high-precision Intensity-Modulated and Image-Guided Radiotherapy (IMRT/IGRT) with concurrent and metronomic systemic chemotherapy to maximize locoregional control while preserving speech, swallowing, and salivary function.

Precision Radiotherapy & Organ Preservation

3D-CRT, IMRT, and IGRT sparing critical adjacent structures (parotid glands, optic apparatus, mandible, and spinal cord).

Platinum-Refractory & Recurrent HNSCC

Phase II clinical evaluation of the oral/ambulatory EMF triplet regimen (Erlotinib, Methotrexate, 5-Fluorouracil) achieving 68.5% disease control.

Liquid Biopsy & CTC Biomarker Surveillance

Serial Circulating Tumour Cell (CTC) isolation at baseline and 3-month intervals for non-invasive progression monitoring.

Patient-Reported Quality of Life

Longitudinal symptom and functional assessment utilizing EORTC QLQ-HN43 multi-item scales in advanced head & neck cohorts.

Featured AIIMS Clinical Evidence
Role of Circulating Tumour Cells (CTCs) & Phase II EMF Regimen in Recurrent/Metastatic HNSCC (ecancermedicalscience, 2022 & 2023)

Demonstrated 88.5% treatment compliance, 45.7% objective response rate, and validated liquid biopsy CTC detection in 80% of platinum-refractory head and neck cancer patients treated at AIIMS New Delhi.
